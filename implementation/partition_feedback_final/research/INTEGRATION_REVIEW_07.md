## Mathematical lead review
For a fixed region R(E,D), let disjoint nonempty S,T be sets of current E-class minima. Each deletion of the union of its classes yields exact equal ordered projections of the same negative and a named positive (positives may coincide). Any separating block must hit both supports, since otherwise projection of that witness equality onto the block contradicts separation. A coarsening of E therefore merges at least one cross pair. Complete lexicographic cross product, normalized endpoints, partitions the region by first true pair and all-false residual. All-false residual has no feasible member but may contain strict coarsenings; cannot use singleton EXACT_NO for it. Ancestor constraints use original endpoint IDs. A global all-pairs threshold is unsound for mixed paths. The two side minima within a current class give its lexicographically least internal normalized cross pair, sufficient for testing a frozen cross-prefix conflict. No witness discovery completeness required; exact witness validity and alternative completeness are required. No rank addition authorized. Mathematical proposal accepted for isolated opt-in development, not production acceptance.
