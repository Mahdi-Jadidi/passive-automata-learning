## Definitions and independent trust boundary

Fix original ordered alphabet A of size n, ordered positive traces X and negative traces Y, and exact sample/scope digest as independently reconstructed by journal/2. Projection pi_B retains original tokens in order, including epsilon. For a complete partition P, C(P) iff for every y in Y there exists a block B in P such that for every x in X, pi_B(y) != pi_B(x). Cost c(P)=n-|P|. A structurally compatible witness is not external ACCEPT. Raw cross-label equality proves structural infeasibility; no negative means the universal condition is vacuous. With zero blocks and at least one negative the existential condition is false.

Independent proof checker consumes only raw sample, exact independently verified scope and immutable proof records. No producer pruning, DSU, cache, ranking, adapter validation or audit_journal imports. RGS labels are first-occurrence block indices; existing canonical owner arrays use minimum original member. Compare partitions by equivalence relation, not by treating one representation as the other. Root mandatory equality E0 must be reconstructed by independent journal replay and applicable closed structural evidence, not trusted from producer counters. Root contains every compatible partition; it need not contain every incompatible partition. Raw-only proof cannot authorize bounded/operational optimum.

## Region algebra and coverage theorem

Region R(E,D) = {P : E refines P and every original-ID pair in D lies in distinct P blocks}. E is an equivalence relation on the entire alphabet, D branch-local; canonicalize using current E representatives only after validating original endpoints. An E-connected disequality is a checked empty region. All region states must derive from their parent by the recorded operations, never from a producer snapshot alone. E-classes may merge, D constraints persist along that branch and never leak to siblings.

For ordered alternatives L=(e_1,...,e_t), e_i=(a_i,b_i) with distinct valid IDs, define q_i(P) = [a_i and b_i belong to the same P-block]. For child j use E_j=equivalence closure(E union {e_j}), D_j=D union {e_i : i<j}. Also define residual R_0=R(E,D union L). Then R(E,D) is the disjoint union of R_0 and all R(E_j,D_j). Proof: every P in R either has no true q_i, placing it in R_0, or has a unique first true index j, placing it in child j; conversely each child/residual satisfies parent constraints, and two distinct first indices or a first index and no true index are mutually exclusive. Empty children are legitimate only if independently shown empty. For t=0 the residual is the whole parent.

Eliminate R_0 ONLY with an independently proved necessary conflict disjunction C(P) => OR_i q_i(P) for every P in R. A sound primitive: same negative y and two disjoint nonempty supports S,T each a union of E-classes, with positive witnesses x_s,x_t such that deletion outside S and outside T respectively yields identical ordered remainders with y (pi_(A minus S)(x_s)=pi_(A minus S)(y), similarly T). Every separating block must intersect both S and T, else its projection equals the corresponding positive witness. Thus some cross-support pair of E representatives is joined. Use the COMPLETE Cartesian alternative family, or prove every omitted pair impossible in R. All support sets, same negative ID, positive IDs and raw deletion comparisons must replay independently. An empty deletion support is a raw label conflict. Different negatives cannot be substituted for the same-negative premise. Additional dead-cover, alternative-family and rank rules are not enabled until separately specified/proved/reviewed; an unrecognized reason never closes a region.

Generic fallback: use all unordered pairs of distinct E-classes, in a fixed order; all strict coarsenings of E satisfy some q_i. The residual is exactly {E} if E satisfies D, otherwise empty. If E is literal structural NO, residual can be closed with independently replayed failed negative and matching positive witness for every block. If E is UNKNOWN, keep the exact representative pending; if compatible, keep a checked feasible incumbent or account for it by a valid cutoff. Opaque BOUNDED_NO is not structural infeasibility and not admissible in this contract. No canonical merge-parent restriction, symmetry pruning, unused-symbol elimination or heuristic omission without a separate coverage theorem.

## Leaf obligations and optimum theorem

Allowed closure reasons initially:
- EMPTY: independently reconstruct contradiction between E and D.
- RAW_LABEL_CONFLICT: raw identical opposite-label trace, applies globally.
- EXACT_NO: region is singleton E (e.g. all distinct E-component pairs disequal) and literal structural NO.
- CONFLICT_RESIDUAL: independently validate necessary disjunction and reconstruct no-true residual.
- COST: independently verified incumbent P* satisfies C(P*), U=c(P*); every P in R has c(P)>=n-|E|. With one-optimum mode close only if n-|E|>=U; with all-optima mode close only if n-|E|>U. Additional rank bounds disabled pending separate proof.
- EXACT_FEASIBLE: singleton, raw structural witness and correctly accounted incumbent. In all-optima mode record every feasible singleton at final minimum, with dedup by canonical partition. A feasible representative alone does not close a nonsingleton region unless a cost proof separately does.

The base lower bound follows since merging E classes cannot increase block count. Never double-charge mandatory unions. For a finite proof rooted in E0, if all children and residuals are covered and every terminal region is independently empty, incompatible, correctly accounted feasible, or nonimproving against a verified incumbent, no compatible partition with cost below final U exists. Since the incumbent itself is compatible, U is the structural optimum. If no incumbent and all leaves are certified empty/incompatible, structural infeasibility follows. For all-optima enumeration, equality is not a cutoff; exact witness set must equal all feasible partitions of final cost U. A proof of only a lower bound, or journal integrity alone, is not this theorem.

## Serializable proof/frontier obligations (proposed grammar, pending core review)

Header: contract version, raw sample digest, regime/family/capacity digest, pinned root journal digest and verified mandatory owner, objective and one/all-optima mode. Bind every record to its parent path and immutable scope; canonical original ID pairs, explicit alternative order, reason/evidence IDs and all required witnesses. Strict schema/type/range validation, duplicate JSON key rejection, canonical bytes/digests, no producer-computed proof decisions. A node record declares SPLIT, LEAF or OPEN; children/residual derived independently; each path has exactly one disposition. Root path unique, reject duplicate/orphan/reparented records and contradictory state. Evidence may only refer to previously verified immutable evidence under matching scope; recheck region-local applicability. Record independent checked incumbent witnesses and costs; final incumbent must not be worse than any incumbent used in a cutoff (or validate all cutoffs directly against final U). Incumbent is structural only.

Lazy generation: checkpoint records immutable parent and ordered L, emitted prefix count k (0<=k<=t), emitted children's subtrees, residual disposition, and the ungenerated suffix union. Suffix union is represented symbolically as first true index j>k, equivalently parent plus first k disequalities plus at least one later equality. Do not replace that union with a single arbitrary child or forget the no-true residual. At k=t suffix is empty. A cursor advance must atomically replace exactly the next suffix portion by child j and shrink suffix; replay checks the previous cursor/hash and parent scope. OPEN nodes, nonempty suffix, unresolved residual and UNKNOWN singleton remain frontier tasks; no status based on counters alone. Restart validates full checkpoint state and coverage invariant before new work. Resume must not silently discard pending tasks or use a different alternative order. A chain digest detects accidental mutation, not external authenticity. No durability claim until separately tested.

Independent replay reconstructs the partition of root into all terminal closed regions and unresolved frontier, including virtual children and residuals. Sound closure uses only allowed reasons. A syntactically valid incomplete artifact may have verified local facts but never a complete optimum/infeasibility result. Completion requires no unresolved nonempty region, checked root and checked final incumbent or full infeasibility proof. UNKNOWN is not NO. Resource timeout, memory exhaustion, missing chunks, unavailable evidence or unsupported proof rules prevent completion; malformed/contradictory evidence is INVALID. Define separate evidence_integrity and search_status in eventual schema rather than conflating journal VERIFIED with search completion. Outer contract returns VERIFIED only for a checked complete structural claim; INVALID for established contradiction/forgery; INCOMPLETE for unresolved or censored checking, no certified complete result. Operational status always INCOMPLETE. Enforce fail-closed under python -O using explicit checks, not assert.
