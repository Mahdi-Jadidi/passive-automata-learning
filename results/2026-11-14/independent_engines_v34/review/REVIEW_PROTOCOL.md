# Independent review protocol — V34 logic and hypergraph

Freeze each submitted design, proof and declared dependencies by SHA-256 before review; never edit author-owned files. Review independently against INDEPENDENCE_CONTRACT.md and the V32/V33 foundations. Record exact paths, hashes, assumptions, proof obligations, verdict and scoped sources. Verify niche technical assertions against original papers or official primary documentation; a source's existence does not prove equivalence to this model. Do not imply an implementation or benchmark exists. Output review in English with short Persian explanation and ask: What is the biggest overlooked issue?

1. Extract the actual proposed algorithm, not merely an encoding or name of a solver. Check independence from previous solver engines, all external dependencies and whether a checker is distinct from solving. Mark undecided dependency claims OPEN.
2. Verify criterion quantifiers ∀n ∃B ∀p, both conversion directions, finite construction and exact maximum-block objective. Audit epsilon, empty alphabet, empty P/N, contradictions, unused opaque IDs and duplicate normalization. Construct concrete counterexamples when a stated implication fails.
3. For logic examine labeled-slot occupancy and nonempty blocks, symmetry, auxiliary gate equivalence, satisfiability and optimizer proof/certificate verification, and whether decoded solutions can be lifted back without extra models. Distinguish threshold queries from a single objective and SAT/UNSAT proof requirements. A decision-preserving reduction alone is not a count-preserving reduction.
4. For hypergraphs examine all possible block generation, symbol exact cover and negative at-least-one coverage, overlapping rejection witnesses, nonempty minimal rejecting sets (especially P empty), and negative labels. Multiple witnesses or Algorithm X branching histories must not be counted as distinct partitions. Exact cover of all negative tags is generally too restrictive. Include construction/solution/certificate and exponential output costs.
5. Separately assess feasibility, optimality, infeasibility, C/O, canonical two witnesses and ordered factor/rejector serialization; identify exact proof and certificate for each claimed output. A certificate verifier must itself be specified and justified; partial or interrupted results are not exact claims. Ensure baseline comparisons use identical output contracts and include preprocessing/reconstruction/verification.
6. Record PROVED, REFUTED-SPECIFIC or OPEN per precise assertion. Give concrete defect and counterexample where applicable; at most two focused repair rounds per defect, then OPEN with reason. Re-review changed hashes; preserve earlier verdict history. No blanket rejection of a family or claim of impossibility from a failed representation. No code, benchmark or tests before proof gate.

When author documents are not frozen and present, publish the contract and protocol only, report awaiting the manager, and do not guess their formulations. Biggest overlooked issue: a sound-looking mapping can overcount duplicate representations or omit coverage, and a solver's claimed optimum is not independently verified without a sound complete optimality certificate.
