## Scoped findings

1. **PROVED — finite-sample criterion and objective.** In every consistent AND factorization, each factor accepts each positive projection; a negative needs one block where its projection is absent from all positive projections. Conversely the finite languages H(B) realize every partition satisfying this condition. Thus the quantifier order is ∀n ∃B ∀p. Since active slots correspond exactly to blocks, maximizing their count preserves the objective; threshold k*+1 excludes all larger solutions, and k*=m has the elementary bound m. This says nothing about physical ownership.

2. **PROVED — equality circuit.** For the displayed recurrence, induction on i+j handles (a) unselected last u symbol, (b) selected last u and unselected last v, (c) both selected with equal terminal atomic IDs. The empty-prefix bases are correct; repeated IDs reuse the same selection variable, as required. Constants, including false ID comparisons, must be encoded as fixed constants. For each negative the active-slot disjunction of conjunctions of negated Eq gates is exactly the criterion, including empty conjunction for P=∅. An inactive slot cannot witness rejection. Polynomial gate size follows from the product of prefix lengths over pairs and slots; the complexity statement includes input reading/ID comparison and a polynomial cardinality construction.

3. **PROVED — canonical assignments and conditional unique lift.** Exactly-one membership and the predecessor clauses force the first use of slot k−1 before the first use of k, recursively. Conversely first-occurrence numbering of any partition satisfies those clauses and is unique. Each active and equality/coverage/cardinality auxiliary has exactly one value **provided every introduced gate, including constants and the chosen cardinality construction, has a full equivalence and no unconstrained auxiliaries**. Merely saying “Tseitin” is insufficient if a later encoding uses one-way clauses. Partition blocking on y is correct even without unique auxiliary lifts. A block is nonempty by construction; unused declared IDs remain assigned.

4. **PROVED conditional on a complete correct SAT decision procedure — optimization, C, O and canonical witnesses.** Threshold queries cover 0…m and a maximum exists in the feasible case. Each blocking clause excludes precisely one canonical partition; finite exhaustion counts every compatible partition once. Repeating at equality k* gives O. Retaining the least two vectors over all completed models produces the canonical RGS first witnesses; canonical RGS comparison is numeric lexicographic comparison in declared alphabet order, and blocks are output in slot order. H(B) and first rejecting block follow by deterministic direct projections in normalized input order. This is a mathematical algorithm, not an independently checked count certificate and not a verified implementation. A naive complete Boolean DPLL branches on finitely many variables and terminates absent resource exhaustion; a returned SAT assignment can be directly checked. All enumeration and stored blocking clauses can be exponential, up to Bell(m) partitions; counts require arbitrary precision.

5. **PROVED — model edge cases, subject to input validation.** With m=0 the single empty partition accepts ε; it is feasible exactly when N is empty (then C=O=1, k*=0), otherwise infeasible. For m>0, P=∅ or N=∅ makes every partition feasible. A common positive/negative word is infeasible; for nonempty Σ and disjoint labels the single full-alphabet block is feasible. Duplicates preserve the mathematical family and must be normalized before ordered witness serialization. Opaque multi-character IDs are compared as whole atomic IDs. These claims do not independently establish the precise C028 schema/error serialization.

6. **OPEN — initial one-optimum certificate contract, including infeasibility.** The design expressly leaves independently checked UNSAT proof/trace engineering open: neither exact byte format and parser, trace production, verification algorithm and acceptance conditions, nor trust boundary and verification resource costs are specified. A SAT answer or the assertion that a correct DPLL exhausted search is not itself a portable independently verifiable certificate. Direct replay proves feasibility only; a feasible k-block partition plus a checked UNSAT certificate for the *precisely reconstructed* F∧(active≥k+1), or the elementary k=m upper bound, would establish optimality. Infeasibility requires a checked refutation of F or a separately specified independently checkable direct proof, including the empty-alphabet case. **Required repair:** select and fully specify a concrete proof format and generator and independent verifier, specify deterministic formula construction/binding and correctness/trust assumptions, invalid or truncated proof handling, finite termination and complexity including worst-case proof size and verification, and INCOMPLETE behavior. A proof format from the literature is an option, not evidence it is implemented or compatible with this proposed DPLL. In particular [DRAT-trim's primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06229) defines a proof format and checker; adopting it requires an explicit binding and dependency decision.

7. **OPEN — historical full C028 implementation contract.** Conditional mathematical counting does not by itself specify and verify all required external status, recommendation, invalid-input/null fields, exact witness serialization, and interruption behavior in an implementation. The proposed arbitrary SAT model order does not preserve C028 `max_candidates` prefix semantics; the author explicitly marks that optional compatibility open. Separate C/O and witness obligations are stronger than a single optimum certificate. A direct replay of selected witnesses cannot validate counts or canonical minimality. **Required repair:** specify the exact C028 API and fields against its authoritative contract, document unsupported budget separately, and provide a complete implementation/verification plan after the proof gate. Do not label solver answers or exhaustion independently certified without a certificate for those claims.

8. **OPEN — operational independence and dependency claims.** The declared future design uses standalone Python-standard-library DPLL, does not call existing partition solvers, and confines old reference to later testing. That is coherent design-level independence, but actual source, runtime version, imported dependencies, verifier isolation and execution paths do not yet exist for audit. The limited PATH/interpreter check cannot establish global unavailability of external solvers. No novelty or speed claim follows.

9. **OPEN — interruption and resource details.** Nulling exact mathematical claims after caught interruption is sound; the mechanism must also cover cancellation, timeout, memory exhaustion, inability to serialize proof, interrupted verification and unknown outcomes, without treating any of them as UNSAT. An uncaught crash cannot guarantee a structured response. Specify which failures can be caught and the API/transport behavior for failures that cannot be caught.

**REFUTED-SPECIFIC:** No mathematical counterexample to the displayed recurrence or canonical-slot formulation was found. No blanket refutation is warranted. The initial certificate and full operational contracts remain OPEN, not silently assumed proven.

## Source scope and largest overlooked issue

[Davis, Logemann and Loveland (1962)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/368273.368557) is primary historical SAT-search background, not a proof of this encoding, implementation, novelty or performance. [Wetzler, Heule and Hunt, DRAT format and DRAT-trim](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06229) is primary material for an example checkable UNSAT-proof format, not a certificate produced by this design. The correctness arguments above are independently derived from the frozen formulas; the sources do not establish them.

The largest outstanding issue is the missing specified and independently checkable UNSAT/optimality certificate, compounded by the separate burden of proving exhaustive counts and canonical witness order. A sound encoding and a feasible model do not close either obligation.
