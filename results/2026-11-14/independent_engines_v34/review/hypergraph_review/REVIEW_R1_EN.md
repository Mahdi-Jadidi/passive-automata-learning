## Verdict

**PROVED — abstract mathematical certificate scheme, subject to explicit branch/bound precedence below.** Independent reconstruction of R(B), feasibility checking, complete least-index branching, and the q+|S| bound support a sound and complete finite certificate of one optimum or infeasibility. This is a mathematical design finding, not approval of the hypergraph engine under the separate fundamental-independence gate, which remains OPEN.

**OPEN — literal verifier semantics:** the node-grammar paragraph says that at S empty and U empty a feasible completion “must cause rejection of a purported no-better/infeasibility proof,” but also permits `bound` at any node with q+|S|≤k, including ties, and explicitly accepts the empty-alphabet optimum by bound. For an OPTIMAL proof a compatible leaf with q≤k must be allowed to close by `bound`; otherwise, e.g. m=0, N empty, k=0, the proposed certificate is rejected. Read the sentence as applying to an uncovered leaf or to an unbounded feasible leaf, not to a valid bound leaf. Amend the specification with tag-directed precedence: first check a `bound` node iff claim is OPTIMAL and q+|S|≤k, including S=∅; otherwise at S=∅ accept only `uncovered` iff U≠∅; at nonempty S accept only a complete `branch`. For INFEASIBLE never allow bound. Reject all other tags and malformed nodes. Under this clarification the existence and induction proofs hold. This is a precise textual defect, not a counterexample to the corrected scheme.

**OPEN — wire format and executable checking.** The abstract finite typed tree and its mathematical predicate are adequate for a mathematical design approval conditional on the above clarification; byte-level interoperability, parser ambiguity handling, precise resource-limits, actual independent implementation and audit remain an engineering/implementation gate. They must be resolved before any real artifact can be called independently verified. The abstract tree is not a deployed serialization, and no tested checker is claimed. If “specified representation” in the milestone is interpreted as requiring an executable wire format before design approval, the milestone remains OPEN under that stricter reading; distinguish that policy choice from the mathematical proof.

## Proof and costs

For a valid OPTIMAL witness, recomputed exact projection coverage establishes a compatible partition with k blocks. In any subtree with q selected blocks and remaining symbols S, every additional block consumes at least one distinct remaining symbol, so a completion has at most q+|S| blocks. If that is at most k, a bound node excludes strictly better completions, including compatible ties. This does not certify uniqueness or counts. At nonempty S, each partition of S has exactly one block containing its smallest index, and enumerating every subset containing that index exactly once exhausts disjoint completions. Recomputing R(B) ensures U records exactly uncovered negative samples. At S=∅, nonempty U proves that completed partition incompatible. Induction on the finite tree proves that an accepted OPTIMAL certificate excludes all partitions with more than k blocks; witness feasibility supplies the lower bound. For INFEASIBLE, no bound is permitted and every leaf must have nonempty U, proving no partition compatible.

Conversely, a true claim admits a finite certificate: expand every required branch, close by a valid bound where possible for OPTIMAL, and close remaining terminal incompatible leaves by uncovered. An unbounded compatible OPTIMAL leaf would have q>k, contradicting optimality. For infeasibility all leaves are incompatible. With m=0, a feasible empty witness closes by bound; if N is nonempty, uncovered closes the infeasible tree. Contradictory labels and P empty are correctly handled by direct reconstruction, as are epsilon, duplicate normalization, and unused IDs.

There are at most 2^m−1 rows. Row construction requires these many times polynomial work in explicit input lengths with exact sequence comparison. The unpruned tree has at most (m+1) Bell(m) nodes: any node extends to an unpruned full partition and each partition has exactly one path with at most m+1 prefixes. The certificate tree is a pruned subtree; its number of branch-pair entries is at most its nodes minus one. A checker must account for generating and comparing all required branch labels, membership/coverage operations, bit-vector and index sizes, projection preprocessing, witness checking, and actual certificate parsing/I/O. Per-node row/subset generation can be exponential and big-integer costs are not constant. Materialized rows require exponential space; a streaming checker still needs its traversal stack and whatever per-frame branch generation state is required, beyond input/certificate buffering. These are finite upper bounds, not a claim of efficient certification. [NIST DLMF](https://dlmf.nist.gov/26.7) defines Bell numbers as partition counts and Bell(0)=1.

The trusted input is the original instance, validated and normalized by the checker; producer labels, supplied hashes and rejection tags cannot replace reconstruction. Reject malformed or incomplete structures; exhaustion, interruptions and checker failures produce INCOMPLETE or REJECT, never verified optimality or infeasibility. A final acceptance requires end-of-tree and end-of-input. The exact API is the proposed V34 envelope; an adapter and full C028 contract remain outside this certificate.
