## Independent reasoning and boundaries

A positive must be accepted by every factor, so each factor contains H(B). A negative can be rejected only if some block's projection is outside H(B); conversely choosing the finite language H(B) on each block realizes every covered negative and accepts every positive. This retains the order ∀ negative ∃ block ∀ positive. Exact symbol cover gives a unique set of disjoint, nonempty blocks covering all declared symbols; including all nonempty subsets gives the inverse mapping. A row with zero negative tags must remain available. Multiple rejecting blocks do not add representations because rows are selected as blocks, not as rejection witnesses.

At any reached state, the deterministic symbol a belongs to exactly one block of any extending target partition. That unique block is available and exactly one branch chooses it. Inductively every target follows a unique path even when later symbol choices depend on the state. Every emitted leaf covers symbols and negative tags; pruning by the union of all currently available rejection sets is sound because every future block is among them. Each recursive step removes a symbol. Thus termination and bijection follow. Arbitrary precision leaf accumulators yield the stated counts and optima at exhaustion, and keeping the two smallest integer RGS vectors works regardless of traversal order. Rejection indices must be computed in canonical block order and projected positive words deduplicated in first occurrence order.

For empty alphabet the only partition is empty, whose conjunction accepts ε: it is compatible iff N is empty. With nonempty alphabet and P empty every row rejects each negative; with N empty all partitions work. If P and N overlap no partition works; if nonempty alphabet and labels are disjoint the single full-alphabet row rejects every negative. Unused IDs still occur in rows and must be covered. Inputs are sequences of atomic IDs, not character strings, with repeated letters preserved and duplicate whole words normalized separately by label. A single rejecting block suffices even when many reject.

The claimed tree bound is sound for this unpruned search and its pruning: each node has at least one full partition extension ignoring coverage, and each full partition has at most one path and at most m+1 prefixes, so nodes ≤ (m+1) Bell(m). Storage and row incidence are exponential; projection preprocessing also depends on input word lengths and exact comparison costs. Counts require O(1+log Bell(m)) bits. Full witness reconstruction and any certificate construction and verification must be accounted separately. NIST defines Bell numbers as partition counts including Bell(0)=1: https://dlmf.nist.gov/26.7. Knuth's original [Dancing Links](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0011047) supports the general depth-first exact-cover background, not this mixed-cover correctness or a speed/novelty claim.

## Concrete repair requests

1. Specify a standalone, finite, tamper-checkable certificate for optimality and infeasibility and its verifier from raw normalized input, including a precise serialized grammar, all branch completeness/pruning checks, soundness and completeness proof, trust and dependencies, preprocessing, size/time/memory upper bounds, and rejection of truncated or malformed certificates. A viable direction is a complete deterministic search tree with every child explicitly accounted for and locally verified terminal/pruning reasons; alternatively a separately specified verifier may reconstruct the exhaustive tree from a compact claim, with its exponential re-solving cost honestly stated. For a feasible optimum independently verify witness and no larger compatible partition; for infeasibility verify no compatible partition, including empty alphabet. Provide correct strict threshold and empty-set conventions. A certificate need not be polynomial, but its costs and whether the checker effectively solves the instance must be explicit.
2. Specify exactly what output is produced on invalid input, interrupted generation and interrupted checking; never report a claim as certified before checking finishes. State unknown and hard-kill behavior, and separate construction, checker and solver dependencies. Bind a verification result to the exact instance and candidate; do not rely on an unverifiable “search completed” flag.
3. Specify RGS as integer tuples with numeric lexicographic order and provide full field-by-field mapping against the actual C028 interface, especially statuses, recommendation, counts, ties, empty and invalid cases and budget. If prefix budget compatibility is not targeted, qualify full C028 compatibility accordingly. No counts certificate is implied by a certificate of one optimum.

No repairs have been received or reviewed in R0. The decisive issue is the absent independently checkable certificate, not the validity of the row-cover bijection. New bytes require a hash-bound follow-up review; theoretical proof here is not evidence of a correct implementation, physical ownership, practical efficiency, or novelty.
