# Independence and review gate

The proposed engine enumerates candidate-block incidence rows and searches a mixed-cover hypergraph by deterministic uncovered-symbol columns. It does not import, invoke, dispatch to, or fall back to the existing C028 reference, RGS enumeration, subset DP, graph contraction or V33 hybrid engines. RGS is computed only as an output canonical encoding of each emitted block selection, not generated as the search backend. It is an Algorithm-X-style extension: standard exact cover alone does not express reusable at-least-one negative coverage. A separately implemented validator and independent certificate checker may be used if their dependencies are stated; the frozen reference may be used only as a later oracle, never as the engine or fallback. No code, tests, benchmark, public interface or historical result has been changed by this checkpoint.

Correctness claims in DESIGN_PROOFS_EN.md require independent proof/contract review before any implementation. The reviewer should particularly examine dynamic-column uniqueness, coverage-union pruning, negative overlap, empty alphabet, exact counts and first-two full-RGS comparisons, replay serialization, resource and budget semantics, and the claimed node bound. Any identified defect gets at most two focused repair rounds, retaining evidence; if unresolved mark OPEN with cause. Finite resource/timeout or NP-hardness does not prove impossibility of an exact solver. Equal sample-compatible output does not establish physical ownership.

Preprocessing enumerates exponentially many candidate blocks and incidence relations; the design makes no claim of a fundamentally new mathematical problem, general polynomial runtime, empirical speed, or superiority over V33. It is structurally independent in its solving backend, while sharing the unavoidable projection predicate and prior mixed-cover formulation. Source: Knuth, [Dancing Links](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0011047) (exact cover background); V32 package B documents the mixed-cover reduction; neither establishes performance of this proposed adaptation.
