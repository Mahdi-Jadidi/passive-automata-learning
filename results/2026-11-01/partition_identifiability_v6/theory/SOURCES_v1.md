# C006 primary-source scope (access 2026-11-01)

1. Henry, Mousavi, Neele, Sammartino, *A Detailed Account of Compositional
Automata Learning through Alphabet Refinement*, arXiv:2504.16624v3.
[Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.16624v3). Consulted finite observation
projection consistency framework (Proposition 5/Corollary 12, also used in prior
cycles). We specialize to disjoint independent factors and unrestricted regular
languages. No novelty claim for the finite projection witness criterion.

2. Komenda, Masopust, van Schuppen, *On Conditional Decomposability*, author
preprint arXiv:1201.1733v2, 2012.
[Primary PDF](https://iccl.inf.tu-dresden.de/w/images/1/1c/On_conditional_decomposability.pdf).
Checked introduction's separability definition and section 2's inverse-projection
parallel composition. Whole-language equality to a product of projected languages
is distinct from consistency with finite labels. Coordinator-alphabet extensions
and shared events in that work are outside our disjoint-alphabet task. We do not
transfer its conditional-decomposability algorithm/complexity claim to unknown-k
sample partition inference.

3. Berstel, Boasson, *Shuffle factorization is unique*, TCS 273 (2002), 47–67,
DOI 10.1016/S0304-3975(00)00433-3. Prior-cycle reading concerned normalized
multisets of word factors of a whole finite shuffle set, not arbitrary regular
factors or finite labeled observations. **Current access limitation:** author PDF
https://www-igm.univ-mlv.fr/~berstel/Articles/2002ShuffleFactorizationUnique.pdf
timed out; publisher https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304397500004333
returned 403. No new full-text reading or theorem verification claimed in C006.
Our proofs do not depend on this unavailable text.
