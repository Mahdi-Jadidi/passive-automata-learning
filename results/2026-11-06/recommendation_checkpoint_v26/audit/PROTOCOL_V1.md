Edge controls include invalid inputs, contradictory epsilon, no samples, no negatives, epsilon-only negative, expired deadlines, memo limits, input renaming, alternating calls, and post-counterexample update on a fresh solve. Oneblock identity and returned negative counterexamples selftest before theorem-specific checks. Check epsilon triples and characteristic-sample convergence once frozen theorem definitions arrive. Never infer convergence from finite word cutoff alone.
