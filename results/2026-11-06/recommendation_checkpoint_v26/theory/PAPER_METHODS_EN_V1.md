# C026 paper methods v1

We receive a finite anonymous alphabet and finite positive and negative complete words. Each symbol belongs to exactly one nonempty block. A block carries an arbitrary regular language, and a global word is accepted precisely when every block projection is accepted. Inputs contain no known component count, device map, target automaton or state bound. This is the C024 AND model, distinct from C025 selected-current-output semantics.

For each candidate block B, compute the negatives whose B-projection is absent from all positive B-projections. A partition is compatible exactly when these rejection masks cover every negative. We recommend a compatible partition with the largest number of blocks, breaking ties by the least canonical restricted-growth string over the sorted alphabet. This is an explicit recommendation convention. We report ambiguity of the entire compatible family separately from ties among maximum-block recommendations. A unique optimum with more than one block remains observationally ambiguous because oneblock and all coarsenings are compatible.

Sound forced relations can first contract symbols to atoms. The optimizer applies a lazy max-plus dynamic program over remaining atoms and uncovered negatives, always finalizing a block containing the least remaining atom. This directly adapts C009's count recurrence. Memo entries hold optimal values, tie counts saturated at two and reconstruction choices. Empty uncovered masks permit the unique singleton-atom completion; negatives not rejectable by the entire remaining union prove a dead state. Resource interruptions preserve only independently replayed feasible incumbents and sound count bounds. They do not turn an incumbent into a proved optimum. Complete proofs, complexity bounds and tie/timeout obligations appear in PROOFS_V1.md.

The first theoretical result shows that every finite compatible partition admits nonempty infinite prime cofinite regular factors. Assign each negative to a block with an unseen positive projection, exclude those assigned projections and one additional unseen word from that block's universal language. A cofinite language projects universally to every proper subalphabet, so its nonuniversality prevents a proper independent decomposition. Thus imposing primeness alone adds no finite-sample ownership information when state sizes are unrestricted. In particular, prime oneblock completions persist for every finite consistent sample.

The second result concerns a fixed nonempty regular complete language L. Its valid disjoint decompositions have a unique finest member Pi_star by the projection/meet-closure argument already developed in C008. Every invalid bipartition has a negative word whose two projections each occur in some accepted word. The negative and two accepted lifts rule out the cut. With Q states in any complete DFA for L, a three-copy epsilon-product has Q^3 states; its shortest accepting path yields all three words with lengths below Q^3. One such triple for every invalid cut gives at most 3(2^(m-1)-1) distinct labelled words. Their compatible partitions are exactly the coarsenings of Pi_star.

Consequently the exact maximum-block recommendation eventually equals Pi_star on an exhaustive passive informant that eventually labels every word correctly. Q is a proof parameter, absent from inference input. Neither the characteristic sample's arrival nor completeness of an arbitrary observed log is certified. Fixed runtime quotas also do not imply exact optimization finishes at each prefix. Physical ownership coincides with the limiting behavioral factorization only under the declared nonempty prime-factor assumptions; named devices remain outside the evidence.

The derivations supply no speed, real-data accuracy or external novelty claim. This checkpoint's mathematical contributions require independent proof review; implementation correctness and timing require their separately frozen audits.
