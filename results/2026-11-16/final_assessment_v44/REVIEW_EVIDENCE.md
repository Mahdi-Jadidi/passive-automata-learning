# Evidence review — V44 assessment

Scope: AI-assisted, read-only review of `ARTICLE_EN.md`, `CLAIM_STATUS.md`, V44 assessment scripts and recorded data, and the frozen V43 encoder, certificate checker, and benchmark harness. This is not independent peer review or formal verification. No benchmark was rerun and no historical results were changed.

## Findings

1. **Evidence validation is a record audit, not a fresh independent replay.** `assess.py` asserts the recorded `pass` flags for 100 encoding instances and the family results, and `summarize.py` asserts recorded `pass`, `checked`, and status fields in the 2,261 audit and 120 benchmark rows. It recomputes the stated count of 15,664 from recorded atom counts and verifies the benchmark source/corpus hashes; it does not rerun each solver, projection check, or certificate from the raw rows. The article describes the historical checks rather than claiming a new replay, but `assessment.json` labels these counts `verified_*`; read that as verification of records and integrity, not independent reproduction. The stored encoding records also omit individual assignment outcomes, so all 15,664 individual assertions cannot be audited from those records alone.

2. **Benchmark comparison is bounded and implementation-specific.** `benchmark.py` uses the same input and checked-output contract, but the enumeration and DP implementations use the V43 encoder and certificate pipeline, while V42 uses its own version; V43 additionally self-checks inside its optimizer. The selected ten synthetic cases and three repetitions support the reported per-case medians only; several sub-millisecond totals are much smaller than the recorded process startup times. No broad speed or memory ranking follows. The article discloses the baseline pipeline, separate startup, synthetic inputs and counter-ranking; preserve these qualifications when quoting results. In particular, the Bell-number separation is against the explicitly defined exhaustive traversal, not a lower bound on all exact algorithms.

3. **Certificate assurance depends on shared trusted code.** `verify_optimal` checks feasibility against original projections and rebuilds atom/edge or threshold information; `check_drup` checks additions by reverse unit propagation and safely retains deleted clauses. The consumer imports the same `encoding.py` as V43, including atom contraction and witness extraction, so a shared error there can affect both production and checking. V44's assignment checks use a separately written projection comparison but the same prepared groups and extracted constraints; they do not independently validate all preprocessing in isolation. The article correctly states this trust boundary and bounded cross-checks. No definite incorrect acceptance or optimum was established in this review.

4. **Novelty and field claims are appropriately qualified, but remain open.** The article does not establish first-in-literature novelty or general empirical superiority, and the field audit reports zero valid whole-trace AND-model trials. Its proof sketches and finite audits are evidence for the specified finite-sample objective, not proof that software is defect-free, that recovered atoms represent physical devices, or that the approach works on real operational episodes. Independent expert literature and mathematical review remain necessary for stronger scientific claims.

## Review limits

This review inspected representative source and verification paths and the aggregation scripts, not every raw record, proof trace, historical implementation, cited publication, or input edge case. No new exhaustive proof checking, performance measurements, or reproducibility run was performed. The article's core structural separation and certificate reasoning appear consistent with the inspected implementation under their stated assumptions, but this review does not certify them universally.
