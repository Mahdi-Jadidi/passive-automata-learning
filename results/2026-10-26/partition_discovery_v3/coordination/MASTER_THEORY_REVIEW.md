# Master theory review for C003

Scope: independent reading of the A/B derivations and source correspondence, not a mechanized proof or a new experiment. Scientific status: verified under the stated assumptions; empirical coverage is recorded in separate accepted audit reports. No novelty claim.

1. General graph certificate: every sound must-link component is contained in each feasible block. Exactly two components and exactly two nonempty blocks leave one unordered partition. Direct projection feasibility establishes existence, which the graph alone does not establish. If a true target belongs to this model, this unique feasible partition is its partition. This is the previously recorded r=k result, applied constructively in C003-GC001.
2. Infinite-family construction: epsilon is positive, each singleton is negative, and each within-block tree edge has a positive two-letter witness. Insertion rescue forces each edge's endpoints together; spanning each true block gives exactly two graph components. The declared local languages are stars of directed tree-edge tiles, so they are regular and infinite. They validate every visible label. The generic solver learns the partition; its finite witness languages are not identified as these infinite target languages.
3. Fixed-word certificate: singleton counts and constant pair projections determine each whole block projection. A varying positive pair forces opposite owners only in the singleton-factor class. A connected bipartite opposition graph forces one unordered coloring; full positive/negative checking establishes its class feasibility. Without this class assumption, positive variation can occur inside a regular factor. Connectedness is sufficient, not necessary; no inference of ambiguity follows from disconnected evidence.
4. Persistent search: every pending ownership prefix is visited at most once and checked against all cuts known when popped. Every cut is globally valid for feasible partitions and excludes its generating infeasible leaf. New cuts cannot invalidate a previously sound prune or remove an unreported feasible answer. Exhaustion therefore justifies none/unique; a first answer or unfinished search does not by itself do so. The fair comparator isolates cut use but shares code, so independent saved-F2 and direct oracles remain required.
5. Fixed-word DFA corollary: for nonempty disjoint-support words of lengths ell0 and ell1, the complete singleton DFAs need ell0+2 and ell1+2 states. The complete global shuffle DFA has (ell0+1)(ell1+1)+1 minimal states: all live prefix-position pairs are reachable, and remaining owned-letter counts distinguish them; all rejection states collapse to one sink. Each owner-position interleaving gives a distinct word, so there are binomial(ell0+ell1,ell0) accepted words even with within-block repetition. The corollary assumes the full shuffle target and complete positive traces, not a restricted scheduler or positive prefixes. It is a theoretical count, not an RPNI experiment or an empirical speedup.

The main decision preserves the difference between an identifying sample, an arbitrary compatible explanation and an externally assumed model class. Additional state bounds, noise, shared symbols and unknown factor count have not been established in C003.
