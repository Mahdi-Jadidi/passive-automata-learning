## Scientific targets

1. GENERAL: find a sample-compatible unordered nonempty two-block alphabet partition for unrestricted regular factors, or certify none. Optionally find a second partition or enumerate a small full set for validation. This is not recovery of a true planted partition merely because one candidate was returned. Avoid the old lazy restart on each answer/cut; use graph-component ownership variables, continuing DFS and globally sound learned cuts. Compare with a FAIR find-first reference, never with a baseline forced to enumerate all answers.
2. FIXED_WORD: additionally assume each local factor is a singleton word language, i.e. every complete positive trace is a shuffle of the same two hidden local words. Prove and implement recovery conditions using positive pair-projection variation. If a pair's projection varies between positives, the symbols must belong to different components in this assumed class. Study whether connectedness of this opposition graph certifies the unique partition up to swapping the sides; check all positives and negatives. Demonstrate constructive sample coverage (e.g. u.v and v.u where each local word contains every symbol of its block). Permit repeated symbols. Insufficient coverage must not be labeled global infeasibility; assumption violation must not be confused with infeasibility for general regular factors. Stronger hypotheses, weaker sufficient conditions and refutations all get records.

The known alphabet is a list of distinct one-character strings, at least two symbols; positive and negative are lists of strings over it; duplicate words are accepted and normalized, contradictions/unknown symbols are invalid. Exactly two nonempty disjoint blocks, noise-free finite labels remain fixed. No RPNI, noise or overlapping symbols in C003. Alphabet cap may differ by experiment, but do not silently truncate unknown symbols. No new dependencies; bundled Python 3.12.14, -B -X utf8.
