# Primary-source check, 2026-11-03 (Asia/Tehran)

Limited targeted review, not an exhaustive novelty search. Exact URLs and
checked portions follow. No secondary search-result claim is relied on.

1. Léo Henry, Mohammad Reza Mousavi, Thomas Neele, Matteo Sammartino,
   *A Detailed Account of Compositional Automata Learning through Alphabet
   Refinement*, arXiv:2504.16624v3.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2504.16624v3
   Read relevant Section 4.1 / Proposition 5 and Section 6.1 consistency and
   counterexample material. Local observation consistency is prior work;
   their active framework and possibly overlapping distributions differ from
   our fixed passive disjoint model. No active-learning speed result transfers.

2. Pascal Baumann, Moses Ganardi, Ramanathan S. Thinniyam, Georg Zetzsche,
   *Existential Definability over the Subword Ordering*, STACS 2022, LIPIcs219,
   7:1–7:15, DOI10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2022.7.
   https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol219-stacs2022/LIPIcs.STACS.2022.7/LIPIcs.STACS.2022.7.pdf
   Re-read Lemma3.11, printed7:10, PDF page10: binary projections determine
   words. C002 already cited this. Our proof additionally treats unary blocks.

3. Chris Köcher, Dietrich Kuske, Olena Prianychnykova,
   *The inclusion structure of partially lossy queue monoids and their trace
   submonoids*, RAIRO-Theor.Inf.Appl.52(2018),55–86,
   DOI10.1051/ita/2018003.
   https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1051/ita/2018003.pdf
   Checked Section6 / Proposition6.1 projection lemma: dependence-pair
   projections characterize trace equivalence. This supports the trace-monoid
   connection, not a theorem of unique physical factorization from finite logs.

4. Randal E. Bryant, *Graph-Based Algorithms for Boolean Function Manipulation*,
   IEEE Transactions on Computers35(8),677–691(1986),
   DOI10.1109/TC.1986.1676819.
   https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~bryant/pubdir/ieeetc86.pdf
   Author-hosted primary paper; checked abstract/representation discussion.
   Ordered DAG representations offer sharing, with exponential worst cases.
   This is a representation direction, not evidence of C011 performance.

5. Shin-ichi Minato, *Zero-suppressed BDDs and their applications*,
   International Journal on Software Tools for Technology Transfer3(2),
   156–170,2001. Author repository primary PDF:
   https://eprints.lib.hokudai.ac.jp/dspace/bitstream/2115/16895/1/IJSTTT3-2.pdf
   Metadata and introductory set-family representation discussion checked;
   text extraction is partially garbled. Not claimed to have verified every
   algorithm/proof. The 1993 original ZDD paper was found in search but is not
   treated as full-text checked here.

6. Masaaki Nishino, Norihito Yasuda, Shin-ichi Minato, Masaaki Nagata,
   *Dancing with Decision Diagrams: A Combined Approach to Exact Cover*,
   AAAI31(1),2017, DOI10.1609/aaai.v31i1.10662.
   https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/10662
   Publisher primary abstract/metadata read: exact-cover search with ZDD memo
   sharing. No reproduction or transfer of its reported speedups to our task.

7. Yu Nakahata, Jun Kawahara, Shoji Kasahara, *Enumerating Graph Partitions
   Without Too Small Connected Components Using Zero-suppressed Binary and
   Ternary Decision Diagrams*, SEA2018,LIPIcs103,21:1–21:13,
   DOI10.4230/LIPIcs.SEA.2018.21.
   https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SEA.2018.21
   Primary record/abstract and keywords checked: frontier-based compressed
   graph-partition enumeration. Connectivity-constrained graph partitions are
   different from arbitrary trace-compatible partitions; no direct algorithm
   applicability or bounded frontier for our data is established.

Source paraphrases are deliberately narrow. All new reduction proofs and
implementation recommendations are our explicit derivations, not attributed
as results proved by these papers. No local copies of full copyrighted texts
or fabricated download snapshots are asserted; this file records browsing.
