Add one positive certificate anchor to each A/B/C input: for every device, a full a-cycle visits allSstates and returns0; reach a state q where ab differs from ba, apply ab, then a-repair to0. Concatenate these local accepted paths across devices. For each device add the same anchor with ONLY its adjacent ab exchanged to ba. Actual complete-DFA replay must prove these negatives.
