Bounded primary literature check (not a novelty clearance): Henry, Mousavi, Neele, Sammartino, *A Detailed Account of Compositional Automata Learning through Alphabet Refinement*, arXiv:2504.16624v3, 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2504.16624v3 , §4.1 Proposition 5 and §6.1 definition and Corollary 12. Their local-positive-projection counterexample characterizes observation consistency and has the same obstruction on a nonempty disjoint partition. Their distributions can overlap and their full paper concerns active learning of LTSs, so not all assumptions/results coincide with unrestricted finite regular-factor witnesses. The projection criterion must not be marketed as established novel. Moerman, *Learning Product Automata*, PMLR 93:54–66, 2019, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v93/moerman19a.html , is another related primary work on active learning with decomposable Moore-machine outputs. These readings alone cannot establish whether the maximum-count optimization or proof is novel.
