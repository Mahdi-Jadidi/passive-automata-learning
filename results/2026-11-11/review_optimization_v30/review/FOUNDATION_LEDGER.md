Biggest overlooked point: exactness is a property of the full sample-based result and its assumptions, not a guarantee of physical recovery or an error-free execution on every computer. Counts of *all* compatible partitions and of *optimal* partitions are separate obligations; an optimization that preserves only the optimum is insufficient.
