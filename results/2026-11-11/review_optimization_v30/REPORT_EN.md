# C030: proof review and exact optimization results

The existing C028 proof remains valid for its stated finite-sample model and completed mathematical execution. The independent re-review found no counterexample to its projection criterion, RGS enumeration, exact counts, optimum selection, or witness construction. This is a coordinated-AI review, not a formal proof-assistant or human verification, and it cannot establish literal 100% implementation reliability or recovery of the physical generating system. The tested optimization preserves the full C028 result on the reported corpus, but the measurements do not establish a general speedup. **Keep the C028 reference as the default; use the new variants as experimental options.** No public entry point was changed.

## Contract and proof findings

The input is a finite declared ordered alphabet and finite positive and negative sets of finite full words, over opaque atomic string IDs. The model has independent disjoint regular factors, conjunctive acceptance, no supplied K or state limit, and noiseless labels. The declared objective is the maximum number of sample-compatible blocks; unknown words and physical ownership remain unconstrained. Identical complete results include the exact count C of all compatible partitions, the exact count O of maximum-block partitions, maximum block count, all-compatible status, optimal uniqueness, lexicographically first optimal RGS in **declared alphabet order**, and first two all-compatible and first two optimal witnesses. Witnesses preserve first-occurrence positive projection serialization and the first rejecting block for each first-occurrence-deduplicated negative.

The key criterion remains ∀negative n, ∃one block B, ∀positive p: projection_B(n) differs from projection_B(p). Necessity follows because each local factor must accept all positive projections; sufficiency follows by choosing each finite projected-positive language as a regular factor. The original exhaustive lexicographic RGS bijection visits each partition once; its prefix invariants justify C, O, canonical optimum, and witness order. Empty alphabet has one empty partition whose empty conjunction accepts epsilon. The independently reconstructed obligation ledger, including empty input, epsilon, duplicate words, contradictions, malformed records, interruption, replay, and the distinction between all-compatible and optimal uniqueness, is [FOUNDATION_LEDGER.md](review/FOUNDATION_LEDGER.md). It identifies no new mathematical counterexample under the specified assumptions. Its conditional guarantee does not mean that an externally killed process can return a result or that finite tests prove all inputs.

## Two reviewed changes

**A — singleton-compatible full-family certificate.** After complete validation and contradiction detection, with `max_candidates=None` only, test the singleton partition. If it is compatible, every partition is compatible: each rejecting singleton remains inside a block after any coarsening, and projection inequality survives enlarging that block. The method computes C=Bell(m), best=m, O=1, the singleton optimum, and the first one or two global RGS exactly; it constructs and verifies the same witnesses as the baseline. For m=0 the only candidate is empty and passes iff the negative set is empty. If the singleton test fails, the algorithm falls back to enumeration without claiming infeasibility. Any explicitly supplied budget, including zero, bypasses A so the original candidate-prefix budget boundary is preserved. The Bell number is the **number of compatible partitions**, not a claim that those partitions were enumerated.

**B — bounded per-call block memo.** During the original enumeration, store each block's exact negative rejection vector in a fresh 256-entry LRU keyed by tuples of whole IDs in declared order. Cache hits, misses, and eviction recomputation produce the same vector for the fixed normalized sample. The same per-RGS compatibility decision and unchanged lexicographic traversal, counters, tie handling, and witness constructor therefore produce the same full outputs. B evaluates complete vectors and can do more work than the baseline's early-negative exit. Eviction permits later recomputation; there is no guarantee that each block is evaluated only once. The cache limit bounds stored entries, not alphabet size, sample size or the solution family.

The [annotated pseudocode and equivalence argument](optimization/ANNOTATED_DELTA_AND_PRIOR_WORK.md) and [frozen proposal](optimization/PROPOSAL_PROOFS_V1.md) give the proofs, exact edge cases and primary-source comparison. Independent reviews approved [the optimization proof](review/OPTIMIZATION_PROOF_VERDICT.md) and [the candidate source](review/CANDIDATE_SOURCE_VERDICT.md). These techniques adapt existing Bell/Stirling counting, partition enumeration, and block-rejection reuse already related to prior work, including C026; no research novelty is claimed. A is a special-case certificate, and B does not eliminate the general Bell-number enumeration. Exact Bell computation takes O(m²) arithmetic operations, not constant-cost integer arithmetic; bit complexity and output size grow. Python's configured integer-to-decimal digit limit can prevent later JSON or `repr` conversion of a very large exact returned integer. No rounding, fixed-width count, or unconditional serialization guarantee is assumed.

All variants retain the baseline's `INVALID_INPUT`, `INCOMPATIBLE`, `INCOMPLETE`, and `ERROR` distinctions. Budget exhaustion or caught resource interruption returns null mathematical fields rather than partial exactness; a forcibly terminated process may return nothing. Resource use or the precise point of an external interruption can differ between implementations. The same mathematical outputs are guaranteed on normally completed executions within the model, rather than identical physical runtime behavior.

## Independent correctness audit

The frozen [candidate source](optimization/candidate_v1.py) SHA-256 is `7501901c6de5d7bb78ffc60f152b818733e3bd4460c9a655b8d783f384e8fc2b`; the unchanged C028 baseline SHA-256 is `5f60b27447b37ec07f1fd6bce75be6199b898573d4b32e90413e900e3a615a94`. Source review checked unchanged copied baseline helpers and the three full-contract entry points `solve_a`, `solve_b`, and `solve_combined`.

The final [independent audit](audit/AUDIT_REPORT_V2.md) compared each variant with a separate recursive set-insertion oracle on 2,271 unlimited records, including all 2,187 ternary labelings of binary words through length two and 60 seeded cases, and with the frozen baseline for eight explicit-budget and nine invalid-schema/budget checks per variant. It compared complete serialized outputs and replayed witnesses. A 9-ID diagnostic observed 366 LRU evictions and 111 later reinsertions of evicted keys; separate sequential solves checked that cache data did not leak between inputs. In an early-failure diagnostic, baseline used 6 projection calls and B used 10, even though their compatibility result agreed.

The first candidate run's **harness diagnostic** failed because its fixture incorrectly asserted that a baseline-compatible oneblock case was rejected; the candidate source was not changed. The original script and traceback are preserved in the audit directory. The corrected, independently reviewed diagnostic uses the literal record `alphabet=["a","b"]`, `positive=[["a"],["b"]]`, `negative=[["a","b"],["b","b"],["a","b","b"]]`, with singleton partition `[0,1]`. Its rejection vectors are `(false,false,false)` and `(false,true,true)`; the baseline stops at the first negative, while B constructs all vector entries. The v2 suite was frozen and rerun. The v2 result JSON retains a legacy `candidate_calls: 0` field from baseline-only mode; it is not an execution count. The per-variant case counters and audit report record the actual comparisons. Fixture and source hashes, exact commands, both failed and successful evidence, and Python/Windows environment appear in [FROZEN_MANIFEST_V2.json](audit/FROZEN_MANIFEST_V2.json) and the audit report.

## Timing: same full query, not a performance guarantee

After the correctness gate, eight fixed cases were each run three times with the unchanged baseline and combined candidate, identical unlimited budgets and full-output checking: 48/48 process invocations passed. Measurements include fresh-process startup on Windows 11 with Python 3.14.6. Three observations per cell are too few and startup variation too large to support a broad speed claim. Values below are seconds, in execution order, followed by the median; no repetitions or regressions are omitted. Complete outputs and unrounded observations are in [timing_results.json](audit/timing_results.json).

| Fixed case | Baseline: three runs (median) | Combined: three runs (median) |
|---|---|---|
| singleton, no observations, m=8 | 0.4394, 0.5350, 0.4028 (0.4394) | 0.4604, 0.4323, 0.3684 (0.4323) |
| singleton compatible, m=5 | 0.7515, 0.3419, 0.3400 (0.3419) | 0.4490, 0.5481, 0.3529 (0.4490) |
| forced nonadjacent | 0.3994, 0.3802, 0.3770 (0.3802) | 0.4230, 0.3929, 0.3359 (0.3929) |
| tied nontrivial optimum | 0.3982, 0.3744, 0.4077 (0.3982) | 0.3655, 0.3659, 0.5446 (0.3659) |
| repeated masks | 0.4032, 0.3903, 0.4090 (0.4032) | 0.4113, 0.3595, 0.4076 (0.4076) |
| early negative failure | 0.3767, 0.5046, 0.4643 (0.4643) | 0.3708, 0.3652, 0.4578 (0.3708) |
| long words / many traces | 0.3885, 0.6883, 0.4066 (0.4066) | 0.3772, 0.4502, 0.3871 (0.3871) |

The combined median was higher on the m=5 singleton case, forced-nonadjacent case, and repeated-mask case, and lower on the other five; overlapping, variable process-startup measurements do not establish causal or statistically reliable improvements. A-only and B-only were correctness-tested, not separately timed in this matrix, so these numbers cannot attribute time to either change. No memory measurement was performed. The responsible next step is to retain the independently reviewed exact reference as default and offer the candidate only for controlled use; the certificate's proof justifies its result, while the present timings do not justify an automatic performance policy. No old source or public dispatch was changed.

Biggest overlooked point? The sample optimum and unique optimal tie status do not identify the physical system. In addition, exact family counts and ordered witness lists remain part of every comparison, even when a shortcut avoids enumeration.
