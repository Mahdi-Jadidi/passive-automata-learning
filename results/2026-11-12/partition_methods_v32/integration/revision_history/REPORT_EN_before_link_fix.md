# Partition methods: integrated research draft

**Integration release candidate — dossier reviews passed; independent integration review pending.** This document is a synthesis of the linked dossiers, not a solver, benchmark, or claim of physical identification. Status labels apply to the specific stated formulation, not an entire family. PROVED means the accompanying argument establishes the stated result under the model; REFUTED-SPECIFIC identifies a counterexample only to the named procedure; OPEN means no conclusion is established here, not impossibility.

## Model and exact contract

The declared ordered finite alphabet Σ has m opaque atomic IDs, including IDs absent from every trace. P and N are finite sets of complete words, including ε. A partition has pairwise-disjoint nonempty blocks covering Σ; Σ=∅ has one empty partition. For B⊆Σ, write H(B)={π_B(p):p∈P} and R(B)={n∈N:π_B(n)∉H(B)}. Unknown K and state counts are unrestricted. Factors are regular and combined by AND.

**Projection criterion.** Π is compatible exactly when ⋃_{B∈Π} R(B)=N. Every factor must accept all H(B); hence each negative needs one block whose projection matches no positive projection. Conversely the finite regular languages H(B) accept every positive and reject every negative with such a block. In particular, the quantifier order is ∀n∃B∀p. If B⊆D then R(B)⊆R(D): equality after projection to D would imply equality after further projection to B. Therefore every coarsening of a compatible partition is compatible.

C counts all compatible partitions; k* is maximum block count when C>0; O counts optima. If C=0, O=0 and k* is undefined. This draft uses a stronger witness-order contract: exact full output additionally requires the first two compatible and optimal restricted-growth strings (RGS) in declared-ID order when present, deterministic finite factors and first rejecting blocks, replay, and completion evidence. C=1 is sample uniqueness; O=1 is optimal uniqueness; neither alone asserts physical truth. Interrupted computation cannot certify its partial counts. Counts use arbitrary-precision arithmetic.

Contradictory P∩N gives C=O=0. For nonempty Σ and disjoint samples one block is compatible. For Σ=∅ the empty conjunction accepts ε, so the empty partition is compatible exactly if N=∅. For nonempty Σ, P=∅ or N=∅ makes every partition compatible. Unused IDs remain partition elements. These conventions and validation apply to every method. Details and proofs: [A: shared definitions](../package_a/METHODS_1_2_3_6.md#shared-definitions-and-proof-obligations), [B: model](../package_b/DOSSIERS_4_5_9_10.md), [C: model and addendum](../package_c/METHODS_7_8_11_14.md).

## Fourteen families

The separate [comparison](COMPARISON.md) indexes each concrete formulation, guarantee, cost, limitation, and proof anchor. The most direct full-contract baseline is canonical RGS enumeration; the coverage-state DP and exact block-selection formulation also preserve the family, but need separately justified ordering and counting extraction. SAT/MaxSAT/SMT can encode the decision or optimization without explicitly listing all 2^m blocks; labeled slot symmetry and auxiliary variables must be handled when counting partitions. Projection of auxiliary variables alone does not eliminate slot permutations; use canonical partition representations or deduplicate decoded partitions. Greedy, stochastic, learned-automaton and clustering proposals can yield replayable candidates, but their specific candidate rules do not provide the full family. Active labels change the dataset, and structural assumptions must be stated as additional restrictions or certificates.

For DP, D(S,U;z) counts partitions of remaining S that cover uncovered negatives U, by number of blocks. For S≠∅ anchor its least symbol and sum zD(S\B,U\R(B);z) over B⊆S containing that symbol; D(∅,∅;z)=1 and other empty-S states are zero. The unique block containing the least symbol gives a bijection and proves the recurrence. Its generating polynomial is an algebraic object, not a polynomial-runtime claim. A conservative O(3^m 2^|N|) transition bound excludes polynomial factors and projection preprocessing. Enumerating full DAG paths and sorting original RGSs gives correct witnesses but can require Bell(m) paths. See [DP proof and bounds](../package_a/METHODS_1_2_3_6.md#2-subset-dp-with-a-coverage-state).

An exact branch-and-bound node has j finalized blocks, remaining S and uncovered U. The bound j+|S| is valid; if U is not contained in R(S), no completion exists. With a feasible incumbent, pruning an upper bound ≤ incumbent preserves k* only; preserving O and the first optimal witnesses requires retaining ties unless their exact contributions are accounted for. Even lower-objective branches may contribute to C and the first compatible witnesses. See [branch-and-bound](../package_a/METHODS_1_2_3_6.md#6-exact-branch-and-bound-anchored-finalized-blocks).

## Five graph scenarios

These scenarios have separate objects and guarantees; no failed direct representation rules out a different gadget or an exact graph algorithm.

1. **Forced pairs/atoms — PROVED as a sufficient certificate.** If a negative is rejected by neither Σ\{a} nor Σ\{b}, every block rejecting it contains both a and b, so all compatible partitions put them together. Transitive closure yields atoms. If the atom partition itself passes compatibility, all compatible partitions are exactly its coarsenings; thus C=Bell(number of atoms), k* equals that number, and O=1. If it fails, no infeasibility follows. Tests use polynomially many projections; first RGSs need independent ordering proof. [Full proof](../package_b/DOSSIERS_4_5_9_10.md#91-forced-pairs-and-atoms--proved-as-an-exact-preprocessing-certificate).
2. **Candidate-block conflict plus coverage — PROVED with marks.** Make a vertex for each nonempty B, join intersecting blocks, and mark covered symbols and negatives in R(B). Maximum independent vertex sets covering all symbols and negative marks correspond bijectively to optimum partitions. Ordinary independent set alone drops coverage. The representation has 2^m−1 vertices and up to O(4^m) edges, plus mark computation; a graph optimum does not automatically count or order solutions. [Proof](../package_b/DOSSIERS_4_5_9_10.md#92-candidate-block-conflict-and-coverage-graph--proved-with-side-constraints).
3. **Minimal rejecting hypergraph — PROVED.** For each n retain inclusion-minimal nonempty T with n∈R(T). By monotonicity, a nonempty B rejects n iff it contains one such T. Require a partition to contain a witnessing hyperedge within a block for each n, maximizing blocks. Enumerating all candidate subsets is exponential; count partitions, not hyperedge witnesses. Empty P requires nonempty-minimality even when R(∅)=N. [Proof](../package_b/DOSSIERS_4_5_9_10.md#10-minimal-rejecting-hypergraph--proved).
4. **Classical cover/independent set/coloring/clique cover/cut — PROVED restricted reduction; general direct simplification not established.** For a graph G=(V,E), E≠∅, take Σ=V, N={ε}, and P containing the two-endpoint word for every edge. A block rejects ε iff it hits every edge. A compatible partition has a vertex-cover block, so k*=α(G)+1=|V|−τ(G)+1: a minimum cover plus singleton complement attains it, and any cover block bounds the other blocks by its independent complement. This polynomial construction establishes NP-hardness for general instances, not impossibility of exact computation. For bipartite G, matching/cover duality gives this restricted family's k* in polynomial time; it does not automatically give C, O, or canonical witnesses. Coloring, clique cover and cut require separate proved transformations; no universal such reduction is asserted. [Proof and scope](../package_b/DOSSIERS_4_5_9_10.md#93-classical-cover-independent-set-coloring-clique-cover-cut--proved-restricted-reduction-refuted-specific-as-a-general-direct-simplification).
5. **Auxiliary nodes — PROVED polynomial decision reduction; OPEN for the stated single-instance parsimonious objective.** A polynomial circuit compares selected projections and checks coverage and a threshold; gate-equivalent CNF reduces to a clause-occurrence independent-set graph. Satisfiability is equivalent to an independent set with one consistent literal per clause. Repeated threshold queries recover k* with exact graph solving; graph solving may be exponential and literal choices and slot permutations overcount partitions. A single standard unweighted graph whose optimum and counts preserve the whole contract is not established here. [Construction](../package_b/DOSSIERS_4_5_9_10.md#94-auxiliary-node-encoding--proved-polynomial-decision-representation-open-as-a-standard-unweighted-graph-objective).

The restricted graph reduction is derived in the dossier, rather than attributed to the classical papers. The [Kőnig original-paper translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03780) establishes bipartite matching/cover equality; its use does not establish partition counts. The prior local [complexity proof](../../polynomial_partition_v27/complexity/COMPLEXITY_PROOF.md) gives an independent sample reduction and the NP-complete threshold argument. These complexity barriers do not prevent finite exact algorithms.

## Interpretation and sources

The Bell number counts set partitions, including Bell(0)=1 ([NIST DLMF](https://dlmf.nist.gov/26.7)); lexicographic partition enumeration is established combinatorial background ([Stamatelatos and Efraimidis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07472)). The projection obstruction is related to active LTS learning with potentially overlapping distributions, but that model differs from this passive disjoint-factor objective ([Henry et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.16624v3)). [Angluin's original paper](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~lreyzin/papers/angluin87.pdf) specifies membership and equivalence queries for learning a regular language, not physical partition recovery. [Dhulipala et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/dhulipala21a.html) study edge-weighted hierarchical graph clustering, not the sample-partition objective. The criterion and constructions above are verified derivations from the stated model; no novelty or complete literature-search claim is made.

The largest recurring overlooked issue is that proving an optimum value or replaying one feasible candidate does not prove C, O, canonical first witnesses, or physical ownership. Even complete knowledge of Σ* does not distinguish one universal factor from universal singleton factors.
