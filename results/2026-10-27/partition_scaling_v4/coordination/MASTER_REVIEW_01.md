# First master review — C004 Stage 1

This is a proof/code review, not independent experimental acceptance. Source hashes are supplied by the branch readiness records; final audit acceptance remains required.

1. **Positive opposition for arbitrary known k.** Under singleton local languages and a nonempty positive set with matching per-symbol counts, same-block pair projections must be invariant. Conversely invariant unary/binary projections determine the full projected word by occurrence-rank ordering. Thus proper surjective k-colorings characterize positive-compatible partitions. A complete k-partite opposition graph has one unordered k-coloring. The proof in structural/PROOFS_S001.md checks out under these assumptions. Connectedness alone is not a uniqueness certificate for k>=3; the four-symbol path example has three k=3 partitions.
2. **Long-word extraction.** In structural/singleton_solver_v1.py each occurrence is appended to its m-1 incident pair buckets. This constructs exact binary projections in O(mL+m²) per word with O(mL+m²) working storage. This is an asymptotic bound, not an empirical speedup claim. Deadline granularity and output serialization still matter.
3. **General-factor must-links.** Adjacent opposite-label swaps and the exact insertion-rescue triple remain sound for any k. For rescue xy positive, xay negative, xaby or xbay positive, the a-owner sees its negative projection in the rescue, and every other factor sees its projection in xy. Distinct owners therefore cannot reject the negative. The proof does not require singleton languages.
4. **Rejected refutation vs genuine implementation defect.** B first proposed P={epsilon,b}, N={a} as a refutation of the rescue lemma. It lacks the required rescued positive ab/ba and is not a counterexample to that lemma. The proposal was retracted and preserved. Separately, B's first deletion index did query the wrong key and could create exactly such an invalid edge. The correct key is the negative word n, not the shortened base: deleting b from the positive rescue must recover n, and the insertion position must be i or i+1. B's independent small oracle and master's source review detected this actual defect before source acceptance. Failed and corrected attempts remain separate. No old-cycle theorem is retracted by this new-code defect.
5. **Bounded local language pruning.** For S subset T, unequal S projections cannot become equal T projections, since erasure back to S would contradict inequality. A partial block with more than q distinct positive projections therefore cannot extend to a cap-q explanation. At a complete partition the observed projection languages supply a size-at-most-q witness if every negative has an unseen projection. This is not an exactly-one-word semantics for empty positive samples, and does not recover unobserved local words.
